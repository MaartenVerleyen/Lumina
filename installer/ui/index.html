Welcome

Set up Lumina

Lumina sorts, searches and culls your photo library on your own PC. This setup installs the app and, if you like, the AI that powers captions, face recognition and smart search.

  • PeopleFinds faces and recognises who's who.
  • Smart searchDescribe a photo and Lumina finds it.
  • Captions and tagsWritten by a vision model on your PC.

Everything runs on this computer. Your photos are never uploaded.
